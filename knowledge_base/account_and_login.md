# BrightNest Home – Account & Login Help
Last updated: February 3, 2026

## Creating an Account
1. Go to brightnesthome.example and click **Sign Up**.
2. Enter your email and create a password.
3. Verify your email using the 6-digit code we send (expires in 15 minutes).

## Password Requirements
- Minimum 10 characters
- At least one uppercase letter, one number, and one symbol

## Resetting Your Password
1. Go to the login page and click **Forgot Password**.
2. Enter the email address on your account.
3. Check your inbox for an email titled "Reset your BrightNest password."
4. Click the reset link. **The link expires in 30 minutes.**
5. Enter and confirm your new password, then log in.

If you don't see the email, check spam and wait 5 minutes before requesting another.
You can request a maximum of 3 reset emails per hour.

## Account Lockout
- After **5 failed login attempts**, your account is locked for **30 minutes**.
- You can unlock sooner by resetting your password.

## Two-Factor Authentication (2FA)
- Enable under **Account Settings > Security**.
- Supported: authenticator apps (Google Authenticator, Authy) and SMS.
- If you lose your 2FA device, contact support with your order number and the email on file. Identity verification takes 1–2 business days.

## Changing Your Email
- Go to **Account Settings > Profile > Email**. A confirmation link is sent to both old and new addresses.

## Deleting Your Account
- Request deletion at privacy@brightnesthome.example. Deletion completes within 14 days. Active orders must be delivered first.
