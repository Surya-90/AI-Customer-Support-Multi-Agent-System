# BrightNest Home – Refund & Returns Policy
Last updated: January 15, 2026

## Return Window
- You may return most items within **30 days** of the delivery date.
- Items must be unused, in original packaging, with all accessories included.

## Non-Returnable Items
- Gift cards
- Items marked "Final Sale"
- Devices that have been registered with a personal account and factory reset is not completed
- Opened software or subscription products after activation

## Defective Items
- Defective products can be returned within **90 days** of delivery for a full refund or free replacement.
- BrightNest covers return shipping for defective items.

## How to Start a Return
1. Log in and go to **My Orders**.
2. Select the order and click **Request Return**.
3. Choose a reason and print the prepaid label (or QR code for drop-off).
4. Ship the item within 7 days of receiving the label.

## Refund Processing
- Refunds are issued to the original payment method.
- Refunds are processed within **5–7 business days** after we receive and inspect the item.
- Your bank may take an additional 3–5 business days to post the credit.
- Original shipping fees are non-refundable unless the item was defective or our error.

## Return Shipping Fees
- Standard returns: a **$6.95** fee is deducted from your refund.
- Exchanges: free return shipping.

## Late Returns
- Requests made 31–45 days after delivery may be approved as **store credit only**, at our discretion.
- After 45 days, returns are not accepted.
