# BrightNest Home – Shipping Policy
Last updated: January 15, 2026

## Shipping Options (United States)
| Method            | Delivery Time        | Cost                         |
|-------------------|----------------------|------------------------------|
| Standard          | 5–7 business days    | $4.99 (free on orders $50+)  |
| Expedited         | 2–3 business days    | $12.99                       |
| Overnight         | 1 business day       | $24.99                       |

## Order Processing
- Orders placed before 2:00 PM Eastern (Mon–Fri) ship the same business day.
- Orders placed after 2:00 PM, or on weekends/holidays, ship the next business day.
- You receive a tracking number by email within 24 hours of shipment.

## International Shipping
- We ship to Canada, the UK, and Australia only.
- International delivery takes 10–15 business days.
- International shipping costs a flat $19.99. Customers are responsible for customs duties and taxes.

## Restrictions
- We do not ship to PO Boxes for Expedited or Overnight orders.
- Orders cannot be shipped to freight forwarders.

## Lost or Delayed Packages
- If your package has not arrived 10 business days after the ship date (US), contact support@brightnesthome.example.
- We will open a carrier investigation and either reship or refund within 5 business days of confirming the loss.

## Address Changes
- Shipping addresses can be changed within 2 hours of placing the order.
- After that, the order may already be in fulfillment and cannot be changed.
