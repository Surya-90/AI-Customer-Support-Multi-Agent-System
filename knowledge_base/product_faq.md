## Warranty
- All devices include a **1-year limited warranty** from the delivery date.
- Extended warranty (+2 years) is available for $19.99 on NestHub and NestCam.
- Warranty does not cover water damage or physical damage.

## Compatibility
- Works with Alexa and Google Assistant.
- Apple HomeKit is supported on NestHub Mini and NestPlug only.
- Devices require a **2.4 GHz Wi-Fi network** (5 GHz is not supported).

## Common Questions
**How do I reset my NestCam 2K?**
Hold the reset button on the back for 10 seconds until the light flashes red, then release.

**Does NestCam store video?**
Yes. Free plan: 24 hours of cloud storage. NestCare Plus ($4.99/month) extends this to 30 days.

**What is the NestHub Mini's power source?**
USB-C, 5V/2A adapter (included). There is no battery.

**Why is my NestBulb not connecting?**
Confirm you're on 2.4 GHz Wi-Fi, turn the bulb off/on 3 times to enter pairing mode (it blinks), then retry in the app.
